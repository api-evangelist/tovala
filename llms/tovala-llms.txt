## About Us

Tovala is a fresh meal delivery service paired with proprietary smart countertop ovens, designed to make home-cooked meals effortless. By combining weekly rotating, chef-crafted meals with scan-to-cook technology, Tovala eliminates shopping, prepping, cooking, and cleaning, allowing customers to enjoy healthy, delicious food with just one minute of prep. The company’s mission is to simplify mealtime for everyone, offering convenience without sacrificing quality or taste.
